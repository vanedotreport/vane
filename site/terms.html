What Vane is

A free website that describes events which already occurred on the Solana blockchain. All of it is derived from public data. You do not need an account, and there is nothing to buy.

It is not advice

Nothing on this site is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Vane does not recommend buying, holding or selling anything, does not forecast prices, and has no view on whether any token is a sensible thing to own. Decisions you make with money are yours.

What the reports claim, and what they don't

A report states observable facts: when a wallet first transacted, what it held, which transactions occurred in which order, and how a pool's reserves changed. It does not claim to know who controls a wallet.

Nothing published here is an accusation. Vane does not allege that any person or organisation committed fraud, broke a law, or acted dishonestly, and its output must not be read or repeated as though it did. Where accounts are listed as having mentioned a token, that records only that a post existed and when — it implies no involvement, payment or coordination, and many such accounts lose money themselves.

Accuracy

The data is produced by software with limitations we publish rather than hide. Parsers can be wrong, chain data can be misread, and detectors carry error rates that are stated with their sample sizes. Every figure cites the transaction signatures behind it so you can verify it independently, and you should if anything matters to you.

Found a mistake? Open an issue on GitHub. Corrections are made in public, and prior versions are not silently rewritten.
