Privacy

Last updated 5 August 2026

This page is short because there is very little to say.

Vane does not collect anything about you

No accounts. No cookies. No analytics, no pixels, no session recording, no fingerprinting, no advertising network, no third-party scripts of any kind. Nothing you type into the site is sent anywhere or stored.

This is a deliberate trade. The analytics that would tell us whether any of this is working are the same analytics we refuse to run, so we simply do not know how many people visit — and that is the correct price for not surveilling them.

What our host sees

The site is served by Vercel, which like any web host processes the connection needed to send you a page — including your IP address — and may retain operational logs. That is infrastructure we do not control and cannot switch off. Their practices are described in Vercel's privacy policy.

Links out

Pages link to GitHub, X, and blockchain explorers. Those are other people's sites with their own practices, and following a link takes you outside anything we control.

Blockchain data

Vane describes public blockchain records. Wallet addresses and transactions are already public and permanent by the design of the network — Vane neither created that visibility nor can remove it. We publish no personal information about anyone, and do not attempt to link wallets to real-world identities.
